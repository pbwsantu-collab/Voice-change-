1 Active → Passive

Subject + verb + object → Object + be + V3 + by + subject
ex She writes a letter. → A letter is written by her.

2 Passive → Active

Object + be + V3 + by + agent → Subject + verb + object
ex The cake was eaten by John. → John ate the cake.

3 Modal verbs

can, must, should, may → modal + be + V3
ex You must finish it. → It must be finished by you.

4 Continuous tenses

Present/Past continuous: is/are/was/were + being + V3
ex They are painting the house. → The house is being painted.

5 Imperative (orders)

Use let or you are ordered/requested
ex Close the door. → Let the door be closed. / You are ordered to close the door.

6 Questions

Wh- / yes-no: invert subject & auxiliary, then passive.
ex Did she finish the work? → Was the work finished by her?
